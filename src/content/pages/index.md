description: My practice is rooted in the tension between fragility and strength. Drawing from my time in the Bard Fine Arts program, I create ceramic objects that mimic the intricate patterns found in the wild—from the woven complexity of a spider’s web to the rhythmic curve of a deer’s rib cage. Whether through a functional bowl or a purely abstract sketch, my work explores the vessel as a biological concept, reimagining bones and natural structures as modern sculptures.

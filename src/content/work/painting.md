## Color, Light, and Mood

This series explores painting as a slow build—layers of color, scraped-back passages, and quiet revisions that leave traces of decision-making on the surface.

![Drawing study one](/img/ROADKIL-painting25.jpg)

Rather than aiming for a single “finished” moment, the work focuses on tension and harmony: soft edges against sharper forms, muted fields interrupted by brighter notes, and compositions that feel both deliberate and open-ended.

![Drawing study one](/img/antler-girl.jpg)
